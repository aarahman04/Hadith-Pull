Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-05. Applies to both this site and the Hadith Pull Android app.

Hadith Pull is a project for reading hadith narrations. It has no accounts, advertising or tracking. Reading and search run on your device.

Information collected

There is no analytics, advertising or in-app crash-reporting software. Search queries, descriptions, reading history and reading choices stay on your device. Only an explicit “Wrong topic?” report sends the limited fields described below.

Network access

Android app: narrations and search are bundled inside the app. The app can send an explicit wrong-topic report; ordinary reading and search do not require a network connection. External links open only when you tap them.

This site: narrations are served from this same domain — there is no third-party API call to show one. Fonts are included with the site too. Links such as "View on Sunnah.com" open only when you tap them. The hosting provider receives the normal requests needed to serve the website.

Data stored on your device

Your bookmarks, folders, recent searches and descriptions, ten previous readings, selected reading source, calendar/occasion settings, daily saying cursor, unread release-note state and display preferences (theme, reading language, saved text size, grade filter) are stored only on your device — in the app's local storage, or in this site's localStorage, depending on which you use. These stay local unless you explicitly export or share a library. Clearing site data removes browser preferences and bookmarks. Platform backup policies may apply to the Android app.

If you choose offline reading, the website, fonts, narrations, categories and search index are saved in your browser's cache on this device. Your browser may remove cached files when storage is low. Export your bookmark library to keep a backup.

Wrong-topic reports

Only when you confirm a report, Hadith Pull queues the narration URN, actual taxonomy topic ID, one optional structured reason, app version and a randomly generated UUIDv4 installation ID. It sends no name, email, narration text, search query, reading history or admin secret. The report service stores those fields and the UTC day, not request IP addresses or user agents in its application database; hosting infrastructure handles the request under its own policies.

The website currently queues reports locally while browser delivery awaits the report service’s allowlisted CORS deployment. A queued label does not mean delivered. Reports expire after 30 days. Once delivery is enabled, transient failures retry while the website is open or when reopened and online; permanent rejections stop retrying. Clearing browser data removes the queue and installation ID. Reporting never removes a topic label immediately.

Reminders and updates

Android notifications are scheduled on your phone, with your permission. The website offers calendar reminders: your chosen times are saved in this browser, and a calendar file is created only when you ask. Importing it into a calendar is your choice and is governed by that calendar's settings and policies.

The website loads its latest version when you are online. Notes from the developer are available in About. The Android app checks for updates through the Google Play Store under Google's terms.

Sharing

When you share, save or copy a hadith card, the app or site hands the image or text to the destination you choose (for example WhatsApp, Instagram, your photo gallery or the clipboard). What happens next is governed by that destination's own policies. On Android 8 and 9, saving a card asks for storage permission, used only to write that image.

Exporting bookmarks

Export Library and Share Library create a file that you choose where to save or whom to send it to. Hadith Pull does not upload your library anywhere.

Crash statistics

If you have allowed your device to share usage and diagnostics with Google, Google may give the developer anonymous crash and performance statistics for the Android app through Google Play Console. The app itself contains no crash-reporting code.

Children

Both are intended for a general audience. The same local-storage and optional report rules apply to every reader; there is no account or age-based profile.

Changes

Any update to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
