About

The Idea

Read, Reflect, Remember.

This project was built in my free time as a simple way to read and reflect on Hadith. The goal is to keep it clean, minimal, and focused on the message: no clutter, no noise, just the text.

Every narration is shown together with its full reference: the collection, the hadith number, the chapter, and the grading reported by the source. That way anyone reading it, or receiving a card you shared, can go back and cross-check it for themselves.

Choose En or ع at the top of Read, or press L, to switch reading languages. English starts with the translation; Show full Hadith reveals the complete text together with the Arabic. Arabic mode shows the full Arabic narration, and the site remembers your choice.

Typography

A Note on the Type

English narrations are set in Cormorant Garamond, and the interface in Inter. Arabic narrations, bookmarks and shared cards use Scheherazade New, the clear, rounder face with fuller vowel marks. Noto Naskh Arabic draws the Arabic letter in the language toggle.

Switching languages does not alter the source text. The Indo-Pak style of the printed Mushaf isn't included, because it needs a differently encoded source text and a font with clear terms for redistribution.

Where the Texts Come From

Hadith, translations and grades are taken from Sunnah.com.

Collections

  • Sahih al-Bukhari
  • Sahih Muslim
  • Sunan Abi Dawud
  • Jami` at-Tirmidhi
  • Sunan an-Nasa'i
  • Sunan Ibn Majah
  • The Forty Hadith of al-Nawawi
  • The Forty Hadith Qudsi
  • The Forty Hadith of Shah Waliullah

Collections are sequences, and some entries carry no text of their own, only “the same as above”, or a second chain of narrators for the hadith before it. Pulled out on their own they point at nothing, so they are skipped and another narration is drawn instead.

Each narration links to its page on Sunnah.com, where you can read it in context.

Offline reading

Save the website and all nine collections on this device to read without a connection, just like the app. The download is about 65 MB.

To keep Hadith Pull close, use your browser's Add to Home Screen option. On iPhone, open the site in Safari first.

Notifications

Daily reminders

Choose your times, then add the reminders to your calendar. Your calendar can remind you even when the website is closed. Times follow your local time zone.

After importing, change or remove these reminders in your calendar.
