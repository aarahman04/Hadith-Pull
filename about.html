About

Android app

Hadith Pull for Android Read offline, get daily and fasting reminders, and keep a Hadith on your home screen. Get it on Google Play

The Idea

Read, Reflect, Remember.

This project was built in my free time as a simple way to read and reflect on Hadith. The goal is to keep it clean, minimal, and focused on the message: no clutter, no noise, just the text.

Every narration is shown together with its full reference: the collection, the hadith number, the chapter, and the grading reported by the source. That way anyone reading it, or receiving a card you shared, can go back and cross-check it for themselves.

Choose En or ع at the top of Read, or press L, to switch reading languages. English starts with the translation; Show Full Hadith and reference reveals the complete text together with the Arabic. Arabic mode shows the full Arabic narration, and the site remembers your choice.

Entries that only repeat a previous narration or continue its chain are skipped when drawing, so each result can be read on its own.

Each narration links to its page on Sunnah.com, with the Arabic, translation and source-reported grades. Hadith Pull does not issue its own grading.

Typography

A Note on the Type

English narrations are set in Cormorant Garamond, and the interface in Inter. Arabic narrations, bookmarks and shared cards use Scheherazade New, the clear, rounder face with fuller vowel marks. Noto Naskh Arabic draws the Arabic letter in the language toggle.

Switching languages does not alter the source text. The Indo-Pak style of the printed Mushaf isn't included, because it needs a differently encoded source text and a font with clear terms for redistribution.

Reading and references

Keep the context close

Search for remembered words or describe a narration. Categories let you explore a subject, and Read from keeps your next narration within the topic you choose.

Open the full narration to read both languages and check its reference. The source link takes you to the same narration on Sunnah.com, with the surrounding chapter and reported grades.

Your reading language and bookmarks stay on this device. Export your library from Bookmarks to keep a backup or move it to another browser.

Offline reading

Save the website and all nine collections on this device to read without a connection, just like the app. The download is about 95 MB, including local search.

  • Save once. Keep the collections, category browsing and local search ready in this browser.
  • Stay up to date. Return here to update a saved copy when a new version is available.
  • Keep a backup. Export your bookmarks before clearing browser storage or moving devices.

To keep Hadith Pull close, use your browser's Add to Home Screen option. On iPhone, open the site in Safari first.

Notifications

Daily reminders

Choose your times, then add the reminders to your calendar. Your calendar can remind you even when the website is closed. Times follow your local time zone.

After importing, change or remove these reminders in your calendar.

Dates and occasions

Dates use the offline Umm al-Qura calendar. Adjust them to match local moon sightings. Fasting reminders arrive the evening before and skip Ramadan and prohibited fasting days.

Uses your reminder times above. Import the file into your calendar and check its alerts. Remove an earlier import before adding a replacement.
